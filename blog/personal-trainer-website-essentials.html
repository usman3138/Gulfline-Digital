10 Things Every Personal Trainer Website Needs

TrainersBy Gulfline Digital Team·30 September 2026·3 min read

Dubai is one of the most competitive fitness markets in the region. Clients have endless choice — gyms, boutique studios, apps and hundreds of independent trainers. Your website has a few seconds to show a visitor why you are the right trainer for them. These ten elements make the difference.

1. A clear niche in your headline

"Personal trainer in Dubai" describes thousands of people. "Strength training for women over 40 in Dubai Marina" or "Post-natal fitness coaching, at home or online" instantly tells the right client they are in the right place. A specific niche is easier to remember, easier to refer and easier to rank for on Google.

2. Real photos of you

People buy personal training from a person. Use genuine, well-lit photos of you coaching — not generic stock images of models. A short video introduction is even better. Clients want to know who will be standing next to them at 6 a.m.

3. Qualifications and experience

List your certifications, specialisations and years of experience. If you are registered with a recognised professional body, say so. In the UAE, clients and gyms may ask about your credentials, so being upfront builds trust.

4. Client results and testimonials

Before-and-after stories (always with written consent), strength milestones and short testimonials are your most powerful sales tools. Focus on outcomes that matter to your niche: more energy, pain-free movement, a first 10 km run, confidence after pregnancy.

5. Services explained simply

Describe each option: one-to-one sessions, partner training, small groups, online coaching, nutrition guidance. For each, say who it suits, how long sessions last and what is included.

6. Pricing or package guidance

You do not have to publish every rate, but a starting price or sample package ("10 sessions from AED X") answers the first question most visitors have. Transparent pricing attracts clients who can afford you and saves hours of back-and-forth messaging.

7. Where and when you train

Do you train at a specific gym, in clients' home or building gyms, outdoors at the beach or park, or online? Which areas do you cover — Downtown, JLT, Arabian Ranches, Abu Dhabi? Listing locations helps clients decide and helps your local SEO.

8. One-tap contact and booking

Put a WhatsApp button on every page (see our step-by-step guide). If you use a booking tool for trial sessions, link it clearly. Every extra step loses potential clients.

9. An FAQ section

Answer the questions you hear every week: Do I need to be fit to start? What should I bring? What is your cancellation policy? Do you offer nutrition plans? A good FAQ handles objections before they become reasons not to book.

10. A fast, mobile-first design

Most of your visitors will arrive from Instagram or Google on their phones. If your site is slow or awkward on mobile, they will leave. Keep pages light, images optimised and buttons large enough to tap. Our mobile speed guide explains how.

Bonus: helpful free content

A few genuinely useful articles or short videos — a beginner's warm-up routine, how to train safely in the summer heat, what to eat before a morning session — demonstrate expertise, give people a reason to share your site and support your Google rankings.

What to leave out

  • Exaggerated promises like "lose 10 kg in 2 weeks" — they damage trust and can create legal risk.
  • Autoplaying music or heavy background videos that slow the page.
  • Long walls of text. Use short sections, headings and bullet points.
  • Medical advice outside your qualifications.

Frequently asked questions

What should a personal trainer put on their website?

A clear niche, real photos, qualifications, client results, services and pricing guidance, training locations, an FAQ and an easy WhatsApp or booking button.

Should personal trainers show prices online?

Showing at least a starting price or sample package usually helps attract the right clients and reduces time spent answering price questions.

How can a personal trainer get more clients online in Dubai?

Combine a mobile-friendly website, a complete Google Business Profile with reviews, and consistent social media content that links back to your website.
