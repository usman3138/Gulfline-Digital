Website vs Instagram: Do You Still Need a Website?

StrategyBy Gulfline Digital Team·30 September 2026·3 min read

Plenty of successful coaches, trainers and creatives in the UAE run their whole business from an Instagram profile. So is a website still worth it? The honest answer: Instagram and a website do different jobs. Instagram gets you noticed; your website gets you chosen — and it is the only one you truly own.

What Instagram does well

  • Discovery: reels, hashtags and shares can put you in front of people who have never heard of you.
  • Personality: stories and videos show who you are, which matters in personal services.
  • Social proof: comments, followers and tagged posts show that real people use your service.
  • Direct messages: easy, casual conversation with potential clients.

Where Instagram falls short

You do not own it

Your account lives on someone else's platform. Accounts get hacked, restricted or disabled, sometimes by mistake, and algorithm changes can cut your reach overnight. Years of content can disappear with no one to call.

It is hard to find specific information

A potential client who wants your prices, locations, qualifications and availability has to scroll through posts or send a DM and wait. Many simply move on.

It is weak in Google search

When a parent googles "swimming coach for kids Dubai", Google usually shows websites and Google Business Profiles, not individual Instagram posts. Without a website you are missing people who are actively searching for your service right now.

It can look less established

Corporate clients, schools and higher-paying customers often check for a proper website before they commit. A website with your own domain signals that you are a real, stable business.

The best approach: use both, with the website as your hub

Think of Instagram as the shop window and your website as the shop. A simple system that works for most freelancers:

  1. Post regularly on Instagram to attract attention.
  2. Put your website link in your bio — not a long list of links, just your site.
  3. On your website, answer the questions people keep DMing you about: prices, locations, how sessions work.
  4. Use a WhatsApp button on the website to turn interest into bookings (here is how).
  5. Embed or link your best Instagram content on the website as social proof.

Own your audience

A website also lets you collect things Instagram will not give you: a newsletter sign-up, booking requests with the details you need, and data from Google Search Console about what people search to find you. If your Instagram disappeared tomorrow, your website — and your list of contacts — would still be there.

The verdict

If you are just starting and testing an idea, Instagram alone is a reasonable first step. Once you have paying clients and want to grow, a simple website is one of the best-value investments you can make. It does not have to be big — see what a website really costs in the UAE and what to put on it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Instagram instead of a website?

You can start with Instagram alone, but a website gives you ownership, better Google search visibility and a clearer place for prices and details.

Do Instagram posts show up on Google?

Occasionally, but Google search results for local services are dominated by websites and Google Business Profiles, so relying on Instagram alone means missing many searchers.

What should I link in my Instagram bio?

Link to your own website, ideally a page with your services, prices and a WhatsApp button so visitors can contact you in one tap.
